About this service

Bioacoustic is the Continuous Ecological Monitoring (CEM) application. Recorders left in the field listen for birds, BirdNET names the species in every short window of audio, and the app turns those detections into views for monitoring spots, species and the monitoring network.

Detections are cleaned before anyone reads them. Rain and noise are filtered out, unreliable species are dropped, and the result feeds soundscape indices, daily activity patterns, migration checks and a test of whether birds follow the sun. The full method is under Methodology.

Methodology

How Bioacoustic works

From field audio to bird-activity metrics · BirdNET detection · five-step filtering · acoustic indices

1RecordSong Meter 2, four sites, 2 min on / 4 min off
2Pre-processSegment, denoise, flag rain
3DetectBirdNET, 3-second windows
4FilterFive steps, two clean datasets
5AnalyseIndices, AR, HA, migration, solar

Where it runs

  • CEM is a Docker app (frontend + backend) behind Nginx at /bio-master. The browser only talks to the app.
  • Long processing goes to the shared Airflow + STACD job runner. The app triggers the DAG and polls until it ends in success or failed.
  • On success, results are written under data/bio-master/ and can be browsed in FileBrowser.

Notation used throughout

SymbolMeaning

cs,j,k,h

1 · Study design

  • Four stratified sites at Sanjay Van, Delhi NCR, chosen to differ in vegetation composition, canopy structure and habitat, so comparisons across sites mean something.
  • Song Meter 2 recorders, placed to maximise ambient sound capture and minimise anthropogenic noise.
  • 2:4 duty cycle: 2 minutes recording, 4 minutes sleep. Chosen from a pilot across sites with no measurable loss in species detection or index reliability, and a longer field life.

Full equipment specifications, recording parameters and placement details are in the paper's appendix.

2 · Pre-processing

  • Segmentation: each 2-minute file is one segment.
Tseg = 120 s,   fs = 48,000 Hz
  • Noise reduction: SNR-scaled noise subtraction in the time domain plus spectral gating in the frequency domain, applied through a short-time Fourier transform.
STFT → spectral gate → iSTFT
  • Rainfall removal: heavy rain is broadband noise that inflates the Acoustic Diversity Index. A Random Forest on 40 MFCCs per 10-second chunk labels rain as None / Low / Medium / High, and High segments are dropped before indices are computed.
94.17%overall accuracy
0.96F1 on the Heavy class
3,012chunks from 251 files, 20% held out

Why it matters: without the rain filter, ADI produced false-positive biodiversity signals during the monsoon; with it, index values across the dataset were corrected.

3 · Detection: BirdNET

  • An open-source classifier from Cornell and Chemnitz, trained on more than 6,000 species.
  • Slides a 3-second window over each segment and returns, per window, a species and a confidence in [0, 1].
  • Published weights are used unchanged; nothing is trained here.
  • Every detection becomes one row in the project's aggregate.csv: species, confidence, file, site, date, hour.
cs,j,k,h = #{ d ∈ Ds : site j, day k, hour h }

The confidence floor is a generation parameter. It is set when BirdNET runs. A window below it is never written, so lowering the threshold later cannot recover it. Each site's effective floor is the maximum across its files, and it is recorded in the job log.

4 · Filtering: five steps to a reliable table

StepRule
1 Taxonomic verificationKeep only species on the regional eBird / Merlin checklist.
Sverified = { s ∈ Sraw : s ∈ SeBird }
2 Confidence thresholdDrop low-confidence windows.
Dfiltered = { d ∈ Ds : conf(d) > 0.3 }
3 Minimum activityA species needs enough detections overall.
Cs = ∑j∑k∑h cs,j,k,h > 10
4 Temporal consistencyAnd enough days with substantial activity.
Ks = | { k : Cs,k > 10 } | ≥ 5
5 Rainfall removalHeavy-rain segments excluded before index computation (see Pre-processing).

Cs,k is the daily total for species s, summed over sites and hours.

DatasetSteps and use
Quality-filteredSteps 1–3. The default for every analysis: indices, regularity and affinity.
Temporally filteredSteps 1–4. Used where day-to-day structure matters: migration and solar alignment.

5 · Acoustic indices: the soundscape without species

ADIAcoustic Diversity Index
ADI = −∑i=1N pi log pi,  pi = Ei / ∑j=1N Ej

Shannon entropy of energy across N frequency bands. Higher = energy spread evenly, typical of many vocalising species.

ACIAcoustic Complexity Index
ACI = ∑t=1T−1 |S(f,t+1) − S(f,t)| / ∑t=1T S(f,t)

Frame-to-frame change in amplitude, averaged over bins. High = rapid modulation, like birdsong; low = steady noise.

AEIAcoustic Evenness Index
AEI = 1 − G,  G = ∑i∑j |Ei − Ej| / (2N ∑i Ei)

Complement of the Gini coefficient of band energy. Penalises one band dominating.

NDSINormalized Difference Soundscape Index
NDSI = (EBio − EAnthro) / (EBio + EAnthro)

Biophony (2–11 kHz) against anthrophony (1–2 kHz). −1 is all traffic, +1 is all birds and insects.

Urban masking. At Sites 2 and 3 a high AEI did not mean a balanced soundscape but white-noise saturation from distant traffic. NDSI is what penalises that; no single index is read alone.

6 · Activity Regularity and Habitat Affinity

  • The same idea twice: correlate consecutive days. AR asks when a bird calls; HA asks where. Both use the quality-filtered dataset.
ARActivity Regularity: same hourly schedule day to day?
Xs,j,k = [c1, …, c24]
ρs,j,k = Spearman(Xs,j,k, Xs,j,k+1)
ARs = (1/Ms) ∑j=1Ms ρ̄s,j,  ρ̄s,j = (1/Nj) ∑k ρs,j,k

Hourly profile of day k against day k+1. Pairs are used only when both days have at least one detection.

HAHabitat Affinity: same sites day to day?
Ys,k = [cj1, …, cjm]
ρs,k = Spearman(Ys,k, Ys,k+1)
HAs = (1/Ns) ∑k=1n−1 ρs,k

Per-site counts of day k against day k+1. Pairs are used only when both days vary across sites.

High values mean stable, predictable behaviour, a signature of resource-rich habitat.

7 · Migratory classification

  • Three complementary views of the daily series Cs,k = ∑j∑h cs,j,k,h, on the temporally filtered dataset.
SCISeasonal Concentration Index
SCIs = RSmax,s / ∑k Cs,k
RSmax,s = maxt ∑k=tt+59 Cs,k

Share of all detections inside the busiest 60-day window. Near 1 = migrant-like.

KResidual kurtosis
Ks = (1/N) ∑i=1N ((ri − r̄)/σ)4
rk = Cs,k − Ĉs,k (after a linear trend)

How peaked the residuals are after removing a linear trend. High = a few days of unusual bursts, like an arrival.

PMRPeak-to-Median Ratio
PMRs = Cmax,s / (Meds + ε)
Cmax,s = maxk Cs,k,  Meds = mediank Cs,k

The busiest day against a typical day. Large = extreme spikes relative to normal activity.

Decision rule

migratory ⇔ SCIs > τSCI ∧ Ks > τK ∧ PMRs > τPMR

Thresholds were set by inspecting species with known migratory behaviour, so the criteria separate the two groups cleanly. Everything else is resident.

The silent resident. Coppersmith Barbet scored SCI = 0.97 because it only calls in May. eBird sightings showed it present but silent, so a call-to-sighting check is needed before calling anything a migrant.

8 · Solar alignment: do birds follow the light?

  • Correlate each day's peak calling hour with that day's sunrise or sunset, on the temporally filtered dataset.
  • Solar events are in decimal hours, from site coordinates (φ, λ). Peak hour is taken in each window, Hmorning = {5…9} and Hevening = {17…20}.
SRk = Hoursr + Minutesr/60 + Secondsr/3600,  SSk likewise
Ps,k(m) = arg maxh∈Hmorning cs,h,k,   Ps,k(e) = arg maxh∈Hevening cs,h,k
ΔTsr = Ps,k(m) − SRk,   ΔTss = Ps,k(e) − SSk
ρs(m) = Pearson({Ps,k(m)}, {SRk}),   ρs(e) = Pearson({Ps,k(e)}, {SSk})

The lags ΔT are expected to stay constant if light drives the bird. Correlation is over days with a detection in that window.

ResultReading
High ρ(m)Peak morning calling tracks sunrise as it shifts through the season: dawn drives the bird.
High ρ(e)The same for dusk.
Low ρPeak timing is set by something other than light: food, weather, competition.

Limitations and what they imply

LimitationWhat it means
Acoustics hears calls, not birdsA silent bird is invisible. Seasonal vocal patterns can make residents look migratory; eBird sightings are used as a cross-check.
The confidence floor is permanentDetections below the run's threshold are never written. Sites processed at different thresholds have different floors, flagged in the index.
Timestamps come from filenamesDate and hour are read from SPOT_YYYYMMDD_HHMMSS names. A file that breaks the convention keeps its detections but loses its place in time.
Urban noise distorts indicesTraffic hum inflates AEI; rain inflates ADI. Both are handled (NDSI, the rain classifier) but neither correction is perfect.
Season lengthSCI needs at least 60 days of recordings and the migration rule needs a full season; short deployments can only describe, not classify.
Thresholds are chosen, not learned0.3 confidence, 10 detections, 5 days, and the migration τ values were set by inspection against known species, so they may need re-tuning for a new region.
