DRONE SERVICE

Methodology

From drone imagery to a labelled map of individual tree crowns.

Input

Upload a stitched drone orthomosaic as a GeoTIFF (.tif or .tiff). The image represents your study area and provides the source for crown detection.

How the analysis works

  1. Detect: Detectree2 analyses the imagery in tiles and outlines visible tree crowns.
  2. Review: Crown outlines are combined into a map. Check the detection overlay against the image and adjust settings if trees are missed or merged.
  3. Group: Similar looking crowns are grouped together. Charts and sample crown images help you inspect the groups.
  4. Label: Give each group a species name based on your knowledge of the site. The label is applied to crowns in that group.

Outputs

Download a species map (KMZ), CSV tables, GeoJSON and a STAC record. Each run has a separate results folder in FileBrowser, so you can compare settings and revisit earlier runs.
