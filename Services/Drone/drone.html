About this service

Explore tree crowns in high-resolution drone imagery. Review groups of similar crowns and use local species knowledge to help build a detailed, labelled forest map.

Start with a drone orthomosaic (GeoTIFF) and follow the automated pipeline to segment canopy crowns, inspect AI clusters, and export geospatial layers.

1

4

Methodology

How Drone runs

Drone is a Docker app (frontend + backend) behind Nginx at /drone. When you start a run, the backend triggers an Airflow DAG generated by STACD, polls it until it succeeds or fails, and writes the results to data/drone/, which you can browse in FileBrowser.
