FOREST INTELLIGENCE

Drone

About this service

Explore tree crowns in drone imagery. Review groups of similar crowns and use local species knowledge to help build a labelled forest map.

Start with a drone orthomosaic and follow the application workflow to inspect the analysis and export results.

Documentation ↗ GitHub ↗
Try Drone ↗

Google sign-in is required by the Drone application.

How to use it?

  1. Open Drone and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Create a project and upload your drone GeoTIFF.
  3. Run the analysis, review the clusters and assign species labels.
  4. Finalize and download your results.
